Interactive T-Account Entry System

Double-entry bookkeeping · Narration = the other account

New Entry Debit (Dr)

How it works: Choose the account you are writing in. In the Narration box, type the other account (the contra account). The system will automatically post the opposite entry to that account.
Editing entry — change any field and click Update, or Cancel to discard.
Example: Writing in Cash account, Debit side, Narration = Capital, Amount = 50,000 → System will also credit the Capital account with the same amount and show “Cash” as its narration.
